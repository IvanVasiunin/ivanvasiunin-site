At first he leaned towards system administration, but once he started learning JavaScript and saw the results of his work right away, he knew this was what he wanted to do.

### Why markup and frontend? What does he enjoy most?
He enjoys creating beautiful, elegant designs and writing logic, and seeing the result of his work immediately as he builds it.

### What is he most proud of in his career?
Ivan is most proud of his growth as a specialist: he tries not to stop at what he has already achieved and keeps improving. In terms of concrete results, he is proud of the NumbersValidator app at Liketelecom and of his performance-optimization work at BrainRocket.
