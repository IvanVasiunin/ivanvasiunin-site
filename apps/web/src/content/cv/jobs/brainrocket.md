iGaming platforms: large-scale consumer web products for international markets, across several projects and brands. Team of about 10: developers, QA engineers, a product owner and a team lead.

**Role**

- Implement interfaces from designs: semantic HTML, complex SCSS, responsive layouts.
- Build reusable components and adapt existing ones to new requirements without duplication, keeping in mind how a change affects the rest of the shared system.
- Review code for quality, maintainability, readability, reusability, HTML structure, SCSS, performance and alignment with the architecture.
- Investigate complex UI issues down to the root cause and propose proper fixes instead of workarounds.
- Own frontend performance in my area and propose refactoring where I see technical debt or inefficient code.
- Take part in the full delivery cycle — design, implementation, QA, fixes, release — and support release readiness during critical launches.

**Highlights**

- **Homepage performance.** Traced a poor Lighthouse result to CSS `mask` in buttons repeated across the page, rewrote the components without masks and optimized other heavy gradient components. The team gained a reusable approach to gradient UI that does not hurt rendering.
- **Performance reviews.** Removed unused code, introduced SCSS mixins for reusable styles, optimized images, minimized masks and filters, and fixed layout shift (CLS) during page load.
- **Acting team lead.** Led the team for several weeks during the team lead's absence; the team shipped several important releases to production on time.
- **Mentoring.** Mentored a trainee to Junior level and, over about three months, onboarded three developers who later became a separate team.
- **Promotion.** Joined as a strong Middle and was promoted to Senior through an internal grade review: practical tasks and technical questions from two more experienced colleagues.
